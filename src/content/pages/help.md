# Help & Support

## Account Questions
**How do I open a new account?**
You can open a new account online by visiting our [Apply](/apply) page.

**How do I reset my password?**
Click 'Forgot Password' on the login screen to reset your password.

## Vault Digital Banking
**How do I access Vault?**
You can access Vault by clicking 'Sign in' or navigating to our [Login](/login) page.

## Loans
**How long does a loan application take?**
Most personal loans are approved within 1-2 business days.
