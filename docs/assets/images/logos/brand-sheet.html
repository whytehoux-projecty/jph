Heritage Trust · Identity

An H, with the light left on.

A serif H whose upper counter is an arched doorway, lit from within. The H carries the weight of 138 years; the doorway is the warmth: someone is home. It echoes the lit windows in the homepage photography and the arches of the 1888 building. The glow in the original artwork is drawn here as three flat tones, so it stays crisp at any size and prints in one pass.

Space and size

Clear space. Keep a margin on all sides equal to the width of one stem of the H. Minimum size. Mark 16 px; horizontal lockup 140 px wide; stacked lockup 96 px wide.

Please don't

  • Recolour the doorway. It is always vermilion, or knocked out in one-colour use.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add a glow or shadow.
  • Set the wordmark in another typeface.
  • Place it on a busy photo. Use the reversed version over an Ink scrim.
